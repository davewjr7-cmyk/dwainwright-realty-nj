## Your Source for Real Estate Intelligence in Northern New Jersey

Welcome to the Wainwright Realty Market Insights blog — the place where we break down what's actually happening in the real estate market so you can make smarter decisions.

Whether you're a homeowner thinking about selling, an investor tracking distressed inventory, or a commercial buyer watching cap rate trends, this blog is built for you.

## What We Cover

**Market Updates** — Monthly and quarterly data on median prices, days on market, inventory levels, and transaction volume across Morris County and the surrounding region.

**REO & Foreclosure Intelligence** — We specialize in bank-owned property. We track foreclosure filings, REO pipeline data, and distressed sale trends so our clients see opportunity before the market does.

**Commercial Real Estate** — Industrial vacancy, office absorption, cap rate movements, and investment sales in Northern NJ. We work with both owners and buyers.

**Tips for Sellers and Buyers** — Real, practical advice from a broker who has handled hundreds of transactions — not generic internet content.
